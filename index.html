02

eDNA Taxonomy & Biodiversity

Python TensorFlow VAE HDBSCAN

AI-driven eDNA analysis pipeline for deep-sea biodiversity assessment. Trained a Variational Autoencoder to embed high-dimensional k-mer vectors into genomic representations, then clustered with HDBSCAN to discover unknown species — no reference database needed.

  • VAE for k-mer to genomic embedding transformation
  • HDBSCAN de novo taxonomic identification
  • Delineated 8 known + 2 novel species clusters
Aug – Oct 2025 github.com/d-mahi14/eDNA-project
